US Army Corp of Engineers Campground and RV Park Data Request

In August this year, the Louisville Office of the US Army Corps of Engineers reached out the Kentucky Division of Water and the National Flood Insurance Program to request campground and RV park data for the state of Kentucky. They were working in Indiana and wanted to extend their work into Kentucky.

The request was made to support the development of Emergency Action Plans to establish clear response protocols for flooding for both public and private campgrounds. This effort was motivated by last year's Camp Mystic tragedy.

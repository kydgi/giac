US Army Corp of Engineers Campground and RV Park Data Request

Emergency Action Plans

  • USACE reached out via KY NFIP
  • to identify campgrounds & RV parks
  • and develop EAPs in the event of a natural disaster.

Challenge

  • A list of private campgrounds & RV parks?
  • Ky Department of Tourism
  • maintains such a list.
  • Then, wrangled 344 locations into a point layer.

Compilation

  • Combine with state & federal data (3.9k points)
  • and overlay FEMA DFIRM flood hazard data.
  • Share map and data.

Lessons Learned

  • Interagency collaboration is key.
  • Combinations of data are essential for effective decision-making.

Annual National Land Cover Database & the
Land Cover Explorer

NLCD

  • USGS National Land Cover Database 🔗
  • Nine versions have been created (2001-2021)
  • each version is comparable to the others
  • but now they are legacy data.

Annual NLCD

  • Annual version starting in 1985 🔗
  • uses ensemble of deep learning models
  • each version is comparable to the others
  • but not to legacy data.

KyRaster REST Services

  • New Thematic Services 🔗
  • Project to Ky Single Zone
  • with three raster functions
    • Open, Impervious, and Forested
  • and full-size export capability.
  • LYRX Files 🔗

Raster source files
